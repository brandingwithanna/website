Privacy policy

Who is responsible

Anna Chernoshtan is responsible for the personal information she receives through Branding with Anna. For privacy questions or requests, email anna.chernoshtan@outlook.com.

When you visit

This website is hosted by Vercel. Technical requests can include your IP address, browser information and details needed to deliver and protect the website. The website’s own code does not set advertising or analytics cookies, or store information in your browser. Fonts and photographs are served with the site.

When you get in touch

If you email, call or message Anna, the contact details and information you choose to share are used to respond to your enquiry and manage any resulting work. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information that is not needed for your enquiry. Instagram handles activity on its platform under its own privacy policy.

When you book a call

Calendly loads only after you select “Choose a time”. It handles the name, email address, time and other details you provide to arrange your booking. Calendly supplies its own cookie controls inside the calendar. Its privacy notice explains its processing and cookies.

When you buy a course

Purchase links take you to Payhip. Payhip and the payment provider you select handle checkout, payment and download delivery. Anna receives the customer and order information needed to fulfil the purchase, keep required records and provide support. This website does not collect or store your payment card details.

Why information is used

Enquiries and bookings are handled to respond to your request and take steps towards providing a service. Order information is used to fulfil purchases. Records required by law are kept to meet legal obligations. Technical information is processed for the legitimate interest of maintaining a reliable and secure website. Consent is used where required for optional cookies or marketing; a purchase does not itself subscribe you to marketing.

Storage and service providers

Information is kept for the time needed to handle the enquiry, supply the service, resolve support issues and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. The relevant purpose and legal retention requirements determine how long records need to be kept.

Service providers may process information outside the European Economic Area. Their notices explain their processing, retention and applicable transfer safeguards:

  • Vercel privacy notice — website hosting.
  • Calendly privacy notice — call bookings.
  • Payhip privacy notice — purchases and downloads.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Email anna.chernoshtan@outlook.com to make a request. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, including Italy’s Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.
