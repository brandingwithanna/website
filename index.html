The woman behind the work02 — Meet Anna

I’m Anna.

In my thirties. Survived two immigrations.
Unmarried and living with a dog.

According to patriarchy, I’m a failure.

According to women,
I’m living my best life.

And that’s what I want to help more women achieve: a life they get to choose for themselves.

The background

Psychology.
Marketing.
Lived experience.

I bring a bachelor’s, a master’s, and a postgraduate diploma in psychology, alongside 11+ years of marketing experience.

I’ve worked for and with Versace, Nikki Beach, Vogue, PrettyLittleThing, and other brands on their online presence, social media, and content strategies.

Then I had to apply that experience to rebuilding my own presence after losing Instagram and LinkedIn accounts built over 12+ years.

Experience working for & with

I want you to do well.
And I know how to get there.

My approach brings psychology and marketing together: understanding the woman behind the brand, then helping her communicate her expertise, express her personality, and build an online presence that supports her ambitions.

After more than two decades of learning, studying, and building, I’m here to share what I know and build a community of women creating their own version of success.
